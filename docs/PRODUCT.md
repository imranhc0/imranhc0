# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads and senior engineers at European companies (Berlin, Amsterdam, Stockholm, Zurich and similar). They open the link from a LinkedIn message or a CV, usually on a laptop during working hours, and decide in about a minute whether to book a call. A second audience is fellow engineers reading a blog post someone shared.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Md Imran Hossen, a software engineer building security SaaS (DNS filtering, threat-intelligence pipelines, real-time dashboards). It has to:

- Make the engineering depth obvious fast: what he builds, at what layer, with which tools.
- Show written technical thinking (blog) and concrete case studies (projects).
- State availability clearly: based in Dhaka (UTC+6), open to relocating to Europe or working remotely with CET overlap.
- Load instantly. The site is itself evidence of engineering care.

Success: a reader leaves knowing his specialism, his stack and how to contact him, and the site never feels slow.

## Brand Personality

Precise, load-bearing, calm. The feel of a well-made technical datasheet: exact, structured, confident without shouting. Copy is plain, technical and first-person. No hype words, no buzzword soup.

## Anti-references

- Neon "hacker" dark themes, matrix green, glitch effects.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient blobs, glassmorphism, big-number stat tiles.
- Chip soup: dozens of rounded skill badges with logos.
- Identical card grids for projects and posts.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (italic display serif, drop caps).

## Design Principles

1. Practice what you preach: static, fast, accessible, no trackers. Performance is part of the pitch.
2. Show the system, not adjectives: diagrams, stacks and concrete responsibilities over self-description.
3. Structure is the decoration: grid, numbering and hairlines carry the visual voice.
4. One idea per section: each row of the page answers exactly one question a recruiter has.
5. Honest content: no invented metrics; anything illustrative is labelled as such.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in both light and dark schemes. Visible focus states. All motion is decorative and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Diagrams carry text alternatives. Works without JavaScript.
